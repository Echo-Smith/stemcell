# Operational Work to Product Evidence

Use this reference when a candidate wants to translate operations, public-sector coordination, content work, brand communication, domain practice, or another non-software role into product-relevant evidence.

## Preserve the role boundary

- Keep the formal title visible or pair it with a functional scope after a separator.
- Do not rename operations work as product management merely because product concepts can describe it.
- Separate `transferable product evidence` from `formal product ownership`.

## Model the operating system

Extract evidence through:

`objective → users or stakeholders → supply → workflow → allocation or distribution → feedback → governance → outcome`

For a brand or content system, inspect:

- the audience and perception goal;
- the content or service supply;
- topic taxonomy and prioritization;
- channel-specific distribution;
- contributor and reviewer coordination;
- cadence, monitoring and feedback;
- quality, compliance and sensitive-data boundaries;
- the highest attributable outcome.

## Write without title laundering

Prefer:

- “围绕城市品牌目标，建立选题、审核、平台适配、发布监测与归档闭环”
- “根据平台内容形态与反馈调整分发节奏”
- “作为协同中间层推动团队稿件完成审核与刊发”

Avoid:

- “负责城市品牌产品路线图” when no such ownership existed;
- “通过产品策略提升转化” when only exposure or delivery was measured;
- relabeling uploads, registrations or task counts as growth experiments.

## Attribution test

For each bullet, mark:

- personal action;
- product-like decision;
- team output;
- measured result;
- missing causal evidence.

Use numbers to establish scale or outcome, not to decorate every line. When a person coordinated work created by others, use “衔接、组织、推动、跟进” and state the team nature of the result.
